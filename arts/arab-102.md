**Course Code**: ARAB 102  
**Credit Hours**: 3  
**Faculty**: Dr. Abdulqassoom Al-Khuli, Faculty Staff

**Course Description**:
This course develops advanced reading comprehension and composition skills in Modern Standard Arabic. Through analysis of diverse Arabic texts, students will strengthen their ability to comprehend complex written material and produce well-structured written compositions across multiple genres.

**Learning Outcomes**:
- Comprehend and analyze complex Arabic texts from various genres
- Produce well-organized written compositions in MSA
- Understand stylistic features of Arabic writing
- Apply reading comprehension strategies to academic texts
