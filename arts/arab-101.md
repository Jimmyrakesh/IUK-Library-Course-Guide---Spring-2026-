# ARAB 101 – Principles of Arabic Grammar

**College:** Arts  
**Department:** Arabic Language & Literature  
**Credit Hours:** 3  
**Faculty:** Dr. Mutlaq Al-Murshaid, Dr. Abdulqassoom Al-Khuli

## Course Description
This foundational course introduces students to the principles and rules of
Modern Standard Arabic grammar.

## Learning Outcomes
- Analyze Arabic sentence structure
- Apply grammatical rules in writing
- Identify common grammatical errors

- مبادئ النحو / د. مطلق محمد مبارك المرشاد
